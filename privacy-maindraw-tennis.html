Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MainDraw Tennis for Wear OS handles match information. The app works offline and does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

1. Overview

MainDraw Tennis for Wear OS is an offline tennis scorekeeping application provided by MainDraw AI Tech ltd. The app does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

2. Information Stored on the Watch

To allow an interrupted match to be resumed, the app stores the following information locally:

  • Current points, games and sets.
  • Completed set scores.
  • The selected match format.
  • Undo and redo history.

This information remains in the app's private storage on the watch. It is not transmitted to mAINDRAW AI TECH ltd or any third party.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

MainDraw Tennis does not use:

  • User accounts.
  • Advertising, tracking or analytics services.
  • Location information.
  • Contacts.
  • Photos, camera or microphone access.
  • Cloud storage or external databases.
  • Network-based data transmission.

The app does not sell or share user information.

4. Purchases

Purchases and payment processing are handled by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policies. MainDraw Tennis does not receive or store payment-card information.

5. Deleting Local Match Information

Users can remove saved match information by:

  • Starting a new match.
  • Using End match & exit.
  • Clearing the app's storage through the watch settings.
  • Uninstalling the app.

Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for the saved match state.

6. Children's Privacy

MainDraw Tennis does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or support enquiries, contact MainDraw AI tech ltd at support@maindraw.co.uk.

This policy applies specifically to MainDraw Tennis for Wear OS.
